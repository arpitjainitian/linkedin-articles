# Series 2: "Users will love the chatbot"

The sentence every kickoff says with confidence. Then launch day comes, people try it once, and quietly go back to email and colleagues.

**Theme:** the AI can be accurate and still fail, because of how it feels to use. This series is about trust, conversation design and the human side of AI assistants.

**Level:** L1. No heavy tech; written for product owners, leaders and anyone who designs how people use AI.

**Running example:** the same passport assistant from #JustAddRAG, now opened up to a small team as a travel-desk helper. Real users, real confusion.

**Hashtag:** #UsersWillLoveIt (proposal)

**Overlap with #JustAddRAG:** grounding and adoption were covered from the engineering side there. Here they are covered from the user's side, and the write-ups link back instead of repeating.

## Format (same as #JustAddRAG)

- Curiosity-question titles, numbered from 1, "write-up" naming, no fixed schedule.
- Start with why the topic matters; plain-language analogies with technical names revealed at the end; light "Fix:" pointers.
- Polite humor, real examples from the passport assistant, cheat sheet at the end, three kickoff takeaways.
- Post text in a copy-paste block, with the "you pay for it" impact of the wrong choice.
- Cover: its own iceberg plus an original meme scene and a sticky-note punchline.
