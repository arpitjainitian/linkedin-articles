## Deep dive

**Autonomy is a dial, not a switch.** Levels: suggest only (AI drafts, human submits) → act on small, reversible items → act within limits with human approval above them → act broadly with monitoring. Move up one level only when the checklist score supports it.

**How the checklist maps to the module.** Exact data (2.2), retries (2.3), halfway (2.4), loops (2.5), agent or workflow (2.6), testing (2.7); access, hidden instructions and human approval come from Module 1 (#JustAddRAG write-ups 5 and 8); records from #JustAddRAG write-up 13.

**Rollout pattern.** Start with one repeatable, low-value action (phone bills). Run in "shadow mode" (AI prepares, human submits) and compare. Promote to auto-submit below a limit. Review weekly.

## Capstone lab

Take the expense agent built in this module and:
1. Score it with the checklist.
2. Fix the two weakest items.
3. Re-score and write a one-page autonomy decision: what it may do alone, what needs approval, what it may not do.
