# Lesson 23.94: AI avatar (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain AI avatar with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Record yourself once, speaking for a few minutes. Then type any script, and a video of "you" presents it, lips matching every word, in any language.

## One step further

The AI combines a generated or recorded face, lip movement matched to the words, and a voice (often cloned, write-up 93). Companies use it to make training videos, product explainers and multilingual versions quickly.

Used openly and with consent, it saves time. Used to impersonate someone, it becomes a deepfake (write-up 92).

## Exercise

Explain AI avatar to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for AI avatar? *That's an AI avatar: a presenter who was never filmed for this video.*
2. What is it not? *An AI avatar isn't the person and doesn't think. It reads the script it's given, mistakes included.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Corporate training videos, multilingual marketing clips, and AI news presenters.*

## Glossary

- **Avatar:** a digital character representing a person.
- **Lip sync:** matching mouth movements to speech.
- **Disclosure:** telling viewers it's AI-made.
