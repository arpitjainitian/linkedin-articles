# Lesson 23.1: How to Learn Any AI Word (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can take any unfamiliar AI term and reduce it to a "hello world": the smallest example that shows the idea working.

## The method

1. **Name the buzzword** and write down what you think it means.
2. **Find the smallest example.** One word, one picture, one question. If the example needs a diagram with more than three boxes, it isn't small enough yet.
3. **Add one thing** to get from the toy to the real system.
4. **Find it in a product** you already use.
5. **Only then learn the real term**, and two neighbouring words.

## Exercise

Pick three AI words from a recent meeting or news article. For each, write a one-line hello world and one everyday product where it shows up.

## Quiz

1. Why does this series reveal the technical name last? *So the idea lands before the jargon; the name then labels something you already understand.*
2. What makes a good hello-world example? *It's the smallest case that still shows the whole idea working.*
