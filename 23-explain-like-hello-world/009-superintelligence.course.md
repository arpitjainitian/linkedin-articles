# Lesson 23.9: Superintelligence (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain superintelligence with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

A grandmaster plays chess better than any of us. Now imagine something that out-thinks the best humans not just at chess, but at science, strategy, persuasion, everything.

## One step further

It's the step after AGI (write-up 8). Some researchers think an AI that can improve AI research could speed itself up. Others think progress will stay gradual and bumpy.

The debate is about control: how do you make sure something smarter than you does what you intended? That's the core of AI alignment (write-up 92).

## Exercise

Explain superintelligence to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for superintelligence? *Superintelligence means AI smarter than the best humans in almost every field.*
2. What is it not? *It doesn't exist today, and nobody knows if or when it will. It also isn't "evil AI" by default; the worry is about goals that don't match ours.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *News about AI safety, open letters from researchers, and companies naming superintelligence as a goal.*

## Glossary

- **ASI:** AI beyond the best human ability in most fields.
- **Intelligence explosion:** the idea that AI could rapidly improve itself.
- **AI safety:** work on keeping powerful AI beneficial.
