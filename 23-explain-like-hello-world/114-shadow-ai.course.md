# Lesson 23.114: Shadow AI (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain shadow AI with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

A company bans personal cloud storage. An employee, in a hurry, pastes a confidential contract into a free AI chatbot to summarise it. Nobody approved it, nobody knows, and the contract has left the building.

## One step further

People use AI because it helps them work faster. If approved tools are missing or clumsy, they use personal accounts instead. The risks: confidential data sent to unknown services, outputs nobody checks, and no record of what was used.

Bans rarely work. Most organisations do better by offering good approved tools, clear rules on what data can go where, and simple guidance.

## Exercise

Explain shadow AI to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for shadow AI? *That's shadow AI: AI use nobody approved or knows about.*
2. What is it not? *Shadow AI isn't staff being malicious. It's usually people trying to work faster. The fix is better options, not just blame.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Company emails reminding staff not to paste client data into public chatbots. Security tools that detect AI usage.*

## Glossary

- **Shadow IT:** technology used without official approval.
- **Data leakage:** confidential information leaving the organisation.
- **Acceptable use policy:** rules for what staff may use AI for.
