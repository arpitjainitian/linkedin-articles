# Lesson 23.114: AI-native (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain aI-native with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Two newspapers. One prints the paper and also puts a PDF online. The other was designed for phones from day one: alerts, video, live updates.

## One step further

"AI-added" means an old process with an AI button: the same forms, plus a chatbot. "AI-native" rethinks the process assuming AI is there: maybe the form disappears because the AI reads the document directly, and people review exceptions instead of typing data.

It also shows up in how teams work: small teams using AI for coding, support and analysis as a default, with new roles for checking and owning AI outputs.

## Exercise

Explain aI-native to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for aI-native? *AI-native means built around AI from the start, not AI added later.*
2. What is it not? *Buying AI tools doesn't make a company AI-native. Redesigning work does. (Series 9 covers AI transformation.)*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Startups built around AI from day one, and old products rebuilt so AI does the first draft of everything.*

## Glossary

- **AI-native:** designed around AI from the beginning.
- **AI-added:** an existing product with AI features bolted on.
- **Workflow redesign:** changing the process, not just the tools.
