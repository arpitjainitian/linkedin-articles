# Lesson 23.50: On-device AI (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain on-device AI with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Asking a question to the cloud is like phoning a friend in another city: it needs a connection, takes a moment, and your question travels.

## One step further

It's made possible by small models (write-up 49), quantization (write-up 41) and NPU chips (write-up 48) in modern devices.

Because devices are limited, many features are hybrid: easy tasks run on the device, harder ones are sent to the cloud. Good products tell you which is which.

## Exercise

Explain on-device AI to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for on-device AI? *That's on-device AI: answers without the call.*
2. What is it not? *On-device doesn't always mean private. Check whether a feature quietly sends harder requests to the cloud.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Offline voice typing, photo clean-up, notification summaries and live captions that work in aeroplane mode. My [Today in AI No. 011](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/today-ai-011-text-to-speech-fits-your-phone-internet-needed-jain-1ipuf) covers a text-to-speech model small enough for a phone.*

## Glossary

- **On-device:** running on your own device.
- **Edge AI:** AI running close to where data is created.
- **Hybrid AI:** device for easy tasks, cloud for hard ones.
