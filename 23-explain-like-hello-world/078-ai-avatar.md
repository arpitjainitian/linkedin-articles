# 78. What Is an AI Avatar, the Presenter Nobody Filmed? | AI avatar

![Cover](images/078-ai-avatar-cover.png)

*Explain It Like Hello World series: AI avatar*

"AI avatars." Training videos, ads and news clips now feature presenters who were never filmed for them.

## The hello world

Record yourself once, speaking for a few minutes. Then type any script, and a video of "you" presents it, lips matching every word, in any language.

That's the hello world: an **AI avatar** is a digital presenter, based on a real person or invented, that speaks whatever text you give it.

## One step further

The AI combines a generated or recorded face, lip movement matched to the words, and a voice (often cloned, write-up 77). Companies use it to make training videos, product explainers and multilingual versions quickly.

Used openly and with consent, it saves time. Used to impersonate someone, it becomes a deepfake (write-up 76).

## Where you've already met it

Corporate training videos, multilingual marketing clips, and AI news presenters.

## The real name

An **AI avatar**, also called a **digital human**.

- **Avatar:** a digital character representing a person.
- **Lip sync:** matching mouth movements to speech.
- **Disclosure:** telling viewers it's AI-made.

## What it's not

An AI avatar isn't the person and doesn't think. It reads the script it's given, mistakes included.

## Take this to your next meeting

1. Get consent and contracts before making anyone's avatar.
2. Tell viewers when a presenter is AI.
3. Review scripts as carefully as with a real presenter.

Filmed once, presenting forever.
