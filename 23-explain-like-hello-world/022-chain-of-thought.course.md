# Lesson 23.22: Chain of thought (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain chain of thought with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Ask: "A shop sells pens at 3 for ₹20. How much for 12?" Blurt an answer and you might get it wrong. Write the steps: 12 pens is 4 groups of 3, 4 × ₹20 = ₹80.

## One step further

Remember write-up 11: an AI writes one token at a time. Each written step becomes part of the text it builds on, so it can use its own working, like scribbling on paper.

Researchers found in 2022 that simply asking models to "think step by step" improved results. Reasoning models (write-up 71) are now trained to do this automatically, often in a hidden "thinking" section.

## Exercise

Explain chain of thought to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for chain of thought? *Writing the steps first helps an AI too. That's chain of thought.*
2. What is it not? *The written steps aren't always the real reason the AI chose its answer, and steps can look logical while containing a mistake. Check the answer, not just the working.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Chatbots that show their working on maths problems. "Thinking" sections in newer AI apps.*

## Glossary

- **Chain of thought:** step-by-step reasoning written before the answer.
- **Intermediate step:** one part of the working.
- **Reasoning:** working something out rather than recalling it.
