# Lesson 23.82: Embodied AI (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain embodied AI with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Knowing how to describe making tea is one thing. Picking up a mug without crushing it, filling it without spilling, and carrying it across a cluttered kitchen is another.

## One step further

Language models learned from text. Embodied AI also needs to learn from sight, touch and movement: how objects feel, fall and break. Training happens partly in simulations and world models (write-up 81), then on real robots.

Newer robots use large AI models to understand instructions like "tidy the table", then turn them into precise movements. The physical world is far less forgiving than a chat: a wrong step can break things or hurt people.

## Exercise

Explain embodied AI to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for embodied AI? *Embodied AI is AI with a body, learning the hard part.*
2. What is it not? *Impressive demo videos aren't the same as reliable products. Many are carefully set up or remote-controlled. Ask what works unscripted.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Warehouse robots, robot vacuums, self-driving cars, and demos of humanoid robots folding laundry.*

## Glossary

- **Robotics:** building machines that move and act.
- **Humanoid:** a robot shaped like a person.
- **Sim-to-real:** training in simulation, then on real machines.
