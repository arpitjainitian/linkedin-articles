# Lesson 23.25: Context engineering (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain context engineering with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

A brilliant consultant is only as good as the briefing pack on their desk. Give them the wrong files, too many files, or files out of order, and the advice suffers, however good your question was.

## One step further

Prompt engineering (write-up 20) is about the wording of the request. Context engineering is about everything around it: retrieved documents (write-up 58), memory (write-up 75), tool results, examples, and what to leave out.

In agents, the desk changes every step, so deciding what to keep, summarise or drop becomes the main job. Too little context and the AI guesses; too much and it gets confused and costs more.

## Exercise

Explain context engineering to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for context engineering? *Context engineering is choosing what goes on the AI's desk.*
2. What is it not? *It's not "stuff in everything". More context isn't better context. The skill is choosing the right, small set.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *AI assistants that pull the right files before answering. Coding tools that decide which parts of your code to show the AI.*

## Glossary

- **Context:** everything the AI sees for this answer.
- **Retrieval:** fetching relevant information to include.
- **Summarisation:** shrinking older context to save space.
