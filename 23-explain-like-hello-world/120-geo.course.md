# Lesson 23.120: GEO (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain GEO with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

People used to search Google and click a list of links. Now many ask a chatbot, which reads websites and gives one answer, maybe naming a few sources.

## One step further

AI search tools retrieve pages (write-up 60) and summarise them with citations (write-up 69). Content that's clear, factual, well-structured and from a trusted source is easier for them to use and quote.

Practical steps look a lot like good writing: answer the question directly, include specific facts and dates, keep pages up to date, and make your expertise visible.

## Exercise

Explain GEO to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for GEO? *GEO is making sure that answer can find and quote you.*
2. What is it not? *GEO isn't a trick to fool AI with hidden text. That's close to prompt injection (write-up 104) and can backfire. Clear, trustworthy content is the strategy.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *AI answers in search results and chatbots that name a few websites as sources.*

## Glossary

- **SEO:** search engine optimisation, for ranked links.
- **GEO:** optimising to be used in AI-generated answers.
- **Citation:** when an AI answer names your page as a source.
