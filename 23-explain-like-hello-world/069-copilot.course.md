# Lesson 23.69: Copilot (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain copilot with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

A copilot in a plane suggests, checks and helps, but the pilot stays in charge and decides.

## One step further

Copilots live inside the tools you already use: suggesting the next line of code, drafting an email reply, summarising a meeting. You accept, edit or ignore each suggestion.

Many products are now moving from copilot to agent: from "here's a suggestion" to "I've done it, please check". The more it acts alone, the more checks you need.

## Exercise

Explain copilot to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for copilot? *An AI copilot suggests. You decide. An agent acts.*
2. What is it not? *A copilot isn't a replacement for the person, and its suggestions aren't automatically right. Reviewing them is part of the job.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *GitHub Copilot suggesting code, Microsoft Copilot in Office apps, AI writing help in email. My [Today in AI No. 008](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/today-ai-008-does-using-make-you-give-up-faster-learning-arpit-jain-ppghf/) asks whether using AI makes you give up faster.*

## Glossary

- **Copilot:** AI that assists while a person stays in control.
- **Suggestion:** an output you accept, edit or reject.
- **Human in the loop:** a person reviewing before anything happens.
