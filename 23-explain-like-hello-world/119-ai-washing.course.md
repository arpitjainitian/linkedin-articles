# Lesson 23.119: AI washing (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain AI washing with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

A shampoo labelled "with natural extracts" that contains a single drop. Technically true. Mostly marketing.

## One step further

It happens because AI sells: to customers, investors and job seekers. Simple fixed rules get called "AI", or people quietly do work that's sold as automated.

Regulators have noticed. In March 2024, the US Securities and Exchange Commission settled charges against two investment advisers over misleading claims about their use of AI.

## Exercise

Explain AI washing to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for AI washing? *AI washing is the same trick: AI on the label, little in the product.*
2. What is it not? *Using simple rules isn't bad, and not every product needs AI. The problem is claiming AI that isn't there.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Products suddenly relabelled "AI-powered", and pitch decks with AI on every slide.*

## Glossary

- **Greenwashing:** exaggerated environmental claims.
- **Rules-based:** fixed if-then logic, not learning.
- **Due diligence:** checking claims before you buy or invest.
