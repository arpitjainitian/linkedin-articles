# 99. What Is AI Slop, and Why Is the Internet Filling Up With It? | AI slop

![Cover](images/099-ai-slop-cover.png)

*Explain It Like Hello World series: AI slop*

"AI slop." The new insult for low-quality AI content flooding feeds, search results and inboxes.

## The hello world

A buffet where someone has quietly added hundreds of trays of bland, reheated food. Technically it's food. You just have to dig to find the good dishes.

That's the hello world: **AI slop** is mass-produced, low-effort AI content: articles, images, videos and posts made for clicks, not for value.

## One step further

Generative AI (write-up 6) makes content almost free to produce. Some people use that to churn out endless posts, fake product reviews, filler articles and surreal images aimed at algorithms.

The result: more noise for readers, harder searching, and a risk for future AI trained on it (write-up 40). Platforms respond with labels, ranking changes and detection.

## Where you've already met it

Odd AI images in social feeds, generic "top 10" articles with no real author, and AI-written comments that say nothing.

## The real name

**AI slop**.

- **Slop:** low-quality, mass-produced AI content.
- **Content farm:** a site churning out articles for ad clicks.
- **Signal-to-noise:** how much useful content there is among the junk.

## What it's not

Not all AI-assisted content is slop. The difference is effort and value: a person with something to say, using AI as a tool, isn't producing slop.

## Take this to your next meeting

1. Use AI to help you say something, not to fill space.
2. Add your own experience, examples and checking to anything AI-assisted.
3. Value and reward real expertise in your feeds.

Cheap to make isn't the same as worth reading.
