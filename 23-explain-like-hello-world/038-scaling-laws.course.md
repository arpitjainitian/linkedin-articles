# Lesson 23.38: Scaling laws (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain scaling laws with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Practise piano for 1 hour and you improve a lot. 10 hours, you improve more. 100 hours, still more, but each extra hour adds a little less.

## One step further

The surprise was how predictable it was. Researchers could estimate how good a bigger model would be before building it, so companies kept scaling up.

But each step costs far more for a smaller gain, and good training text is limited. So the focus has shifted: better data, smarter training, and letting models think longer at answer time (write-up 71) instead of only getting bigger.

## Exercise

Explain scaling laws to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for scaling laws? *AI improved the same way. Researchers call it scaling laws.*
2. What is it not? *Scaling laws aren't laws of nature. They're observed trends that held for years, and they don't promise the trend continues forever.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *News about record-sized AI data centres and chips. And headlines asking whether AI progress is hitting a wall.*

## Glossary

- **Scaling:** growing model size, data and compute together.
- **Compute:** the computing power used for training or answering.
- **Diminishing returns:** each extra unit helping a little less.
