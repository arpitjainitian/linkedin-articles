# 22. How Can One AI Read, See and Listen? | Multimodal AI

![Cover](images/22-multimodal-cover.png)

*Explain It Like Hello World series: Multimodal AI*

"Multimodal." Every new model announcement says it. It means more than "has a camera button".

## The hello world

Show an AI a photo of your fridge and ask: "What can I cook tonight?" It names the ingredients and suggests a recipe.

That's the hello world: one AI that takes in **text, images and sound** together and reasons across them.

## One step further

Remember tokens (write-up 2) and embeddings (write-up 4)? Images are cut into small patches, and sound into short slices. Each patch or slice is turned into the same kind of numbers as words, on the same meaning map.

Once everything is on one map, the model can link "red round thing in the photo" to the word "tomato" and to "salad".

## Where you've already met it

Pointing your phone at a sign to translate it. Asking an AI about a chart or a screenshot. Talking to an assistant that can also see your camera.

## The real name

**Multimodal AI**.

- **Modality:** a type of input or output: text, image, audio, video.
- **Image patch:** a small square of a picture, treated like a token.
- **Vision-language model:** a model that understands images and text together.

## What it's not

Seeing isn't understanding perfectly. Multimodal models still misread small print, count objects badly and miss details. Check what matters.

## Take this to your next meeting

1. Try sending a photo or chart instead of describing it.
2. Double-check numbers it reads from images.
3. Ask which inputs a model really supports: text, images, audio, video?

Words, pictures and sounds, all on one map of meaning.
