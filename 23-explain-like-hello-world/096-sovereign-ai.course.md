# Lesson 23.96: Sovereign AI (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain sovereign AI with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Many countries keep their own power plants, even if electricity could be imported. It's about control, reliability and not depending on someone else in a crisis.

## One step further

The reasons: data staying within borders, models that understand local languages and culture, independence from other countries' decisions, and economic opportunity. It can mean national compute centres, home-grown models trained on local languages, or requiring AI to run in-country.

India's IndiaAI Mission, for example, funds computing capacity and Indian language models. In-country AI hosting (my [Today in AI No. 009](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/today-ai-009-same-many-clouds-where-does-your-data-actually-jain-zgc1f)) is a related idea.

## Exercise

Explain sovereign AI to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for sovereign AI? *Sovereign AI is the same idea, for AI models, data and computing.*
2. What is it not? *Sovereign AI doesn't mean shutting out foreign AI. Most countries mix their own capacity with global providers.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *National AI missions, government-funded data centres, and models built for Indian and other regional languages.*

## Glossary

- **Sovereignty:** control over your own resources and decisions.
- **Data residency:** keeping data within a country's borders.
- **Compute:** the computing capacity needed to train and run AI.
