# Lesson 23.106: Responsible AI (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain responsible AI with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Food safety in a restaurant isn't one rule. It's a system: fresh ingredients, clean kitchens, allergy labels, inspections, and someone in charge when something goes wrong.

## One step further

In practice: a list of where AI is used, risk checks before launch, tests for bias (write-up 101) and safety (write-up 98), privacy rules, explanations for decisions (write-up 102), human review where needed, and a way to report and fix problems.

Regulations (write-up 105) turn many of these into legal duties. Standards such as ISO/IEC 42001 describe how to run them as a management system.

## Exercise

Explain responsible AI to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for responsible AI? *Responsible AI is the same idea for AI: a system, with owners.*
2. What is it not? *Responsible AI isn't a principles page. Without owners, checks and records, it's just words.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Company AI principles pages, AI review boards, and risk forms teams fill in before launching an AI feature.*

## Glossary

- **AI governance:** the rules, roles and checks for using AI.
- **AI inventory:** a list of every AI system in use.
- **Accountability:** a named person answers for the outcome.
