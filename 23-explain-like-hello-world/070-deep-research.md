# 70. Can Deep Research Do Your Research for You? | Deep research

![Cover](images/070-deep-research-cover.png)

*Explain It Like Hello World series: Deep research*

"Deep research." Several AI apps launched it: ask a question, wait 10 minutes, get a report. What's it doing in those minutes?

## The hello world

A junior analyst gets a question: "How big is the Indian EV charging market?" They search, read 30 pages, notice gaps, search again, take notes, and write a 5-page report with sources.

That's the hello world: **deep research** is an AI agent (write-up 71) doing that whole loop on its own.

## One step further

Instead of one quick answer, the AI plans sub-questions, runs many web searches, reads the results, decides what's missing, searches again, and finally writes a structured report with citations (write-up 69). It can take minutes and hundreds of pages read.

It's great for a first draft of a research brief. It can still pick weak sources, miss paywalled data or misread numbers.

## Where you've already met it

"Deep research" or "research" modes in major AI chat apps.

## The real name

**Deep research** agents.

- **Research agent:** an AI that plans, searches and reads in a loop.
- **Sub-question:** a smaller question the main one is split into.
- **Report:** the final written summary with sources.

## What it's not

Deep research isn't expert review. Treat the report like a junior analyst's first draft: useful, but check the key numbers and sources.

## Take this to your next meeting

1. Use it to get a sourced first draft fast.
2. Check the most important numbers against original sources.
3. Ask it to list what it couldn't find.

A tireless junior analyst. Still check the numbers.
