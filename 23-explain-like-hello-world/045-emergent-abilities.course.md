# Lesson 23.45: Emergent abilities (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain emergent abilities with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

A child learns words, words, more words... and then one day, they tell a joke. Nobody taught joke-telling directly; it appeared once enough pieces were in place.

## One step further

Researchers noticed that small models failed completely at some tasks, like multi-step arithmetic, while larger models suddenly did well. It looked like a switch flipping at a certain size.

Later research argued some of these jumps depend on how you measure: with a more gradual score, improvement looks smoother. So it's partly real, partly a measurement effect.

## Exercise

Explain emergent abilities to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for emergent abilities? *Emergent abilities: skills that seem to appear once an AI is big enough.*
2. What is it not? *Emergence doesn't mean the AI is secretly conscious or unpredictable in every way. And it doesn't guarantee new skills will keep appearing as models grow.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Headlines about AI "unexpectedly" learning to translate, code or reason as models grew.*

## Glossary

- **Emergence:** a new behaviour appearing from many simple parts.
- **Scale:** model size, data and compute (write-up 44).
- **Threshold:** the size at which a skill seems to appear.
