# Lesson 23.16: Temperature (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain temperature with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Ask an AI to finish "My favourite fruit is ___" five times.

## One step further

**Low temperature:** almost always pick the top-scoring word. Answers are steady and predictable. Good for facts, forms and code.

**High temperature:** give lower-scoring words a real chance. Answers vary and surprise. Good for brainstorming, stories and taglines.

Even at temperature zero, answers can still differ slightly, and other changes (a new model version, different wording) change answers too.

## Exercise

Explain temperature to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for temperature? *Temperature is how adventurous the AI's next word is.*
2. What is it not? *Temperature doesn't make the AI smarter or more accurate. Low temperature makes it consistent, even consistently wrong. (Why answers change after a model update is in #JustAddRAG 14.)*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Pressing "regenerate" and getting a different answer. Creative tools that offer "more creative" or "more precise" modes.*

## Glossary

- **Sampling:** choosing the next word from the AI's scored options.
- **Temperature:** how much randomness goes into that choice.
- **Deterministic:** same input, same output, every time.
