# Lesson 23.81: Multimodal AI (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain multimodal AI with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Show an AI a photo of your fridge and ask: "What can I cook tonight?" It names the ingredients and suggests a recipe.

## One step further

Remember tokens (write-up 11) and embeddings (write-up 13)? Images are cut into small patches, and sound into short slices. Each patch or slice is turned into the same kind of numbers as words, on the same meaning map.

Once everything is on one map, the model can link "red round thing in the photo" to the word "tomato" and to "salad".

## Exercise

Explain multimodal AI to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for multimodal AI? *One AI that reads, sees and listens. That's multimodal.*
2. What is it not? *Seeing isn't understanding perfectly. Multimodal models still misread small print, count objects badly and miss details. Check what matters.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Pointing your phone at a sign to translate it. Asking an AI about a chart or a screenshot. Talking to an assistant that can also see your camera.*

## Glossary

- **Modality:** a type of input or output: text, image, audio, video.
- **Image patch:** a small square of a picture, treated like a token.
- **Vision-language model:** a model that understands images and text together.
