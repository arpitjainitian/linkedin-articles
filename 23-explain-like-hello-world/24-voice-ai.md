# 24. How Does an AI Talk Back Without a Pause? | Voice AI

![Cover](images/24-voice-ai-cover.png)

*Explain It Like Hello World series: Voice AI*

"Real-time voice AI." Assistants that chat like a person, even when you interrupt. How did talking to computers stop feeling like leaving voicemails?

## The hello world

Old voice assistants were a relay race: one system turned your speech into text, another wrote an answer, a third read it aloud. Each hand-off added a pause.

That's the hello world: newer **voice AI** handles hearing, thinking and speaking in one model, so it can reply almost instantly, and even notice when you interrupt.

## One step further

The relay approach (speech-to-text, then text AI, then text-to-speech) still works and is easy to control. But it loses tone, like a sarcastic "great", and adds delay.

Speech-to-speech models turn audio into tokens (write-up 2) directly, reason over them, and produce audio tokens back. They can hear emotion, pause naturally and stop when you talk over them.

## Where you've already met it

Voice modes in chat apps that let you interrupt mid-sentence. Customer service bots that sound surprisingly human.

## The real name

**Voice AI**: **speech-to-speech** models, versus the **ASR, LLM, TTS** pipeline.

- **ASR:** automatic speech recognition: speech to text.
- **TTS:** text-to-speech: text to spoken audio.
- **Latency:** the delay before the reply starts.

## What it's not

A natural voice isn't a sign the AI is more correct. And a human-sounding voice should still tell people they're talking to an AI.

## Take this to your next meeting

1. Test voice bots with interruptions and background noise.
2. Tell callers when they're speaking to an AI.
3. Measure the delay; anything over a second feels slow.

Hear, think and speak in one breath.
