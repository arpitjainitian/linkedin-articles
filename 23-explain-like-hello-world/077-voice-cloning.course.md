# Lesson 23.77: Voice cloning (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain voice cloning with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

A good mimic listens to someone for a while, then speaks in their voice: the tone, the accent, the little pauses.

## One step further

The AI captures what makes a voice distinctive and applies it to new text. Some tools need minutes of clean audio; others claim usable results from seconds.

Uses include dubbing films into other languages in the actor's own voice, and giving a voice back to people who lose theirs to illness. Misuses include fake calls asking family for money.

## Exercise

Explain voice cloning to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for voice cloning? *Voice cloning can copy a voice from a few seconds of audio.*
2. What is it not? *A familiar voice on a call is no longer proof of identity. Don't use voice alone to approve payments or access.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *AI dubbing of videos, audiobook narration, and news of "family emergency" scam calls.*

## Glossary

- **Voice clone:** a synthetic copy of a real voice.
- **Text-to-speech:** turning text into spoken audio.
- **Consent:** permission from the person whose voice it is.
