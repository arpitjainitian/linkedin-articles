# Lesson 23.115: LLMOps (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain lLMOps with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Opening a restaurant is the exciting part. Running it is the real job: stock, hygiene checks, staff rotas, complaints, and noticing when a dish starts going wrong.

## One step further

It covers versioning prompts (write-up 20) and models, running evals (write-up 99) before every change, monitoring quality, cost and speed (write-up 56), tracing what happened in each answer, handling incidents, and rolling back bad changes.

It grew out of MLOps (for older machine learning), with new concerns: prompts, model updates from vendors, hallucinations and token costs.

## Exercise

Explain lLMOps to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for lLMOps? *LLMOps is running the AI restaurant every day.*
2. What is it not? *LLMOps isn't a tool you buy once. It's a practice with owners. (Series 6 covers observability; Series 7 covers demo to production.)*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Dashboards showing AI cost, speed and quality. Teams that can say exactly which prompt and model version gave a bad answer.*

## Glossary

- **Monitoring:** watching quality, cost and speed continuously.
- **Tracing:** recording every step behind an answer.
- **Rollback:** switching back to the last good version.
