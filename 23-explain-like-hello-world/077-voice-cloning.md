# 77. Can Voice Cloning Copy Your Voice From a Few Seconds of Audio? | Voice cloning

![Cover](images/077-voice-cloning-cover.png)

*Explain It Like Hello World series: Voice cloning*

"Voice cloning." Audiobooks, dubbing and assistants use it. So do scammers.

## The hello world

A good mimic listens to someone for a while, then speaks in their voice: the tone, the accent, the little pauses.

That's the hello world: **voice cloning** is AI learning a person's voice from recordings, then saying anything in that voice.

## One step further

The AI captures what makes a voice distinctive and applies it to new text. Some tools need minutes of clean audio; others claim usable results from seconds.

Uses include dubbing films into other languages in the actor's own voice, and giving a voice back to people who lose theirs to illness. Misuses include fake calls asking family for money.

## Where you've already met it

AI dubbing of videos, audiobook narration, and news of "family emergency" scam calls.

## The real name

**Voice cloning**, a form of **speech synthesis**.

- **Voice clone:** a synthetic copy of a real voice.
- **Text-to-speech:** turning text into spoken audio.
- **Consent:** permission from the person whose voice it is.

## What it's not

A familiar voice on a call is no longer proof of identity. Don't use voice alone to approve payments or access.

## Take this to your next meeting

1. Stop using voice alone as a security check.
2. Agree a family code word for emergencies.
3. Get written consent before cloning anyone's voice.

A familiar voice isn't proof any more.
