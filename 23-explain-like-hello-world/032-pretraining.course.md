# Lesson 23.32: Pretraining (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain pretraining with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Before a doctor specialises, they spend years in general medical school learning everything: anatomy, chemistry, how to read and reason.

## One step further

During pretraining, the model plays the next-word game (write-up 12) across trillions of tokens from books, websites and code. It's the costliest step, done by only a few companies.

The result is a base model: it can continue text brilliantly but doesn't yet follow instructions or chat politely. That comes later with fine-tuning (write-up 33) and RLHF (write-up 36).

## Exercise

Explain pretraining to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for pretraining? *Pretraining is the AI's general schooling, before any specialist course.*
2. What is it not? *Pretraining isn't something most companies do. You start from someone else's pretrained model and adapt it.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *The "P" in GPT. Model releases that offer a "base" version alongside a "chat" or "instruct" version.*

## Glossary

- **Pretraining:** the first, huge general training run.
- **Base model:** the result: good at continuing text, not yet at chatting.
- **Corpus:** the body of text it learned from.
