# 69. What Does "Agentic AI" Really Mean? | Agentic AI

![Cover](images/069-agentic-ai-cover.png)

*Explain It Like Hello World series: Agentic AI*

"Agentic AI." "Agentic workflows." The word is on every keynote slide. Is it different from "AI agent"?

## The hello world

Think of a scale, not a switch. At one end: a calculator, which does exactly what you press. At the other: a trusted manager, who decides what to do and does it.

That's the hello world: **agentic** describes how much an AI system decides and acts on its own. More agentic means more freedom over its own steps.

## One step further

A system can be a little agentic (an AI picks which of two tools to call) or very agentic (it plans a multi-day project and acts without asking). "Agentic AI" usually means systems towards the freer end, built around agents (write-up 68).

More freedom means more flexibility, but also less predictability, more cost and more need for limits and checks.

## Where you've already met it

Product launches promising "agentic" features, from email to coding to customer service.

## The real name

**Agentic AI**.

- **Agentic:** acting with some independence towards a goal.
- **Autonomy:** how much it decides without asking.
- **Human in the loop:** a person approving key steps.

## What it's not

"Agentic" isn't a quality badge. More autonomy isn't always better; for routine work, a fixed workflow is often cheaper and safer (Series 2, write-up 6).

## Take this to your next meeting

1. Ask "how agentic?" instead of "is it agentic?".
2. Match autonomy to the risk of the task.
3. Start with less freedom, then widen it as it earns trust.

Agentic is a dial, not a switch.
