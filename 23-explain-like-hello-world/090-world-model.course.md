# Lesson 23.90: World model (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain world model with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Throw a ball in your head. You can picture where it lands before it does. You have a little simulator of the world inside you.

## One step further

Language models predict the next word. World models predict the next moment: the next video frame, or how a scene changes if a robot pushes a box.

They learn from huge amounts of video and interaction. Uses include training robots and self-driving systems in imagined worlds before the real one, and generating interactive game-like scenes.

## Exercise

Explain world model to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for world model? *A world model is an AI that imagines what happens next.*
2. What is it not? *World models don't truly understand physics yet. Watch AI videos closely: hands merge, objects vanish, glasses refill themselves.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *AI-generated videos where objects move realistically (and sometimes don't). Research demos of playable worlds generated by AI.*

## Glossary

- **Simulation:** imitating how the world behaves.
- **Embodied AI:** AI that acts in the physical world, like robots.
- **Physics:** how objects move, fall and collide.
