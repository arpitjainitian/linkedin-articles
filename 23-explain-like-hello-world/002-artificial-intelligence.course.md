# Lesson 23.2: Artificial intelligence (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain artificial intelligence with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

A calculator adds numbers, but nobody calls it intelligent. It follows fixed steps someone wrote.

## One step further

AI is the big umbrella. Under it: rule-based systems (old chess programs), machine learning (write-up 3) where computers learn from examples, and deep learning (write-up 4) behind today's chatbots and image tools.

The bar keeps moving. Once something works reliably, people stop calling it AI. Spell-check and route planning were "AI" once.

## Exercise

Explain artificial intelligence to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for artificial intelligence? *Artificial intelligence (AI) is a computer doing something we'd say needs human intelligence.*
2. What is it not? *AI isn't one technology, and it isn't a mind. "AI-powered" on a label can mean anything from a simple rule to a large language model. Ask what it actually does.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Face unlock, spam filters, maps that reroute around traffic, voice assistants, chatbots.*

## Glossary

- **AI:** computers doing tasks that seem to need human intelligence.
- **Narrow AI:** AI that's good at one kind of task.
- **AI effect:** once something works, people stop calling it AI.
