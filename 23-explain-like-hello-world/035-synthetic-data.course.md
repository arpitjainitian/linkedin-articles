# Lesson 23.35: Synthetic data (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain synthetic data with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

A teacher needs 1,000 maths practice questions with worked answers. Writing them by hand would take months.

## One step further

It's useful when real data is scarce, private or expensive: rare medical cases, new languages, tricky edge cases, or step-by-step reasoning examples.

The catch is quality. If an AI learns only from AI-written data, mistakes and blandness can pile up over generations. So teams check it, filter it, and mix it with real data.

## Exercise

Explain synthetic data to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for synthetic data? *So an AI writes them, and another AI learns from them. That's synthetic data.*
2. What is it not? *Synthetic doesn't mean worthless or free. Unchecked, it can teach errors confidently. It's a tool, not a replacement for real data.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Practice questions in learning apps. Many newer models mention synthetic data in how they were trained, especially for maths and coding.*

## Glossary

- **Synthetic data:** data generated by a model or program, not collected.
- **Data augmentation:** making more examples by varying real ones.
- **Model collapse:** quality dropping when models train mostly on AI-made data.
