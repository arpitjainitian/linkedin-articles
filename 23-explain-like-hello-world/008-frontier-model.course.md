# Lesson 23.8: Frontier model (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain frontier model with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

The edge of a map that explorers are still drawing. Beyond it, nobody has been yet.

## One step further

Frontier models are the newest, largest foundation models (write-up 7) from a handful of labs, trained with huge amounts of computing power (write-up 43 explains why scale helped). They lead on benchmarks (write-up 115) and often get new abilities first.

Compare the two: a foundation model is any large base model that many apps are built on. A frontier model is the newest, most capable of those, right now. Every frontier model is a foundation model, but not every foundation model is at the frontier.

Because they are the most powerful, safety rules and voluntary commitments often focus on them. The frontier also moves: today's frontier model is next year's ordinary one.

## Exercise

Explain frontier model to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for frontier model? *A foundation model is the base. A frontier model is the newest, most capable base, at the edge.*
2. What is it not? *Frontier doesn't mean best for your task. A smaller, cheaper model (write-up 55) often does a simple job just as well.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *The flagship models that AI labs announce with big launch events, and the top tier in a chatbot's model menu.*

## Glossary

- **Frontier:** the current edge of what AI can do.
- **Flagship model:** a lab's most capable model.
- **Compute:** the computing power used to train and run a model.
