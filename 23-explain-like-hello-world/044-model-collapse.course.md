# Lesson 23.44: Model collapse (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain model collapse with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

Photocopy a photo. Then photocopy the copy. Then the copy of the copy. After 20 rounds, details fade and odd smudges grow.

## One step further

Each AI generation slightly over-represents common patterns and loses rare ones. Train the next model mostly on that output, and the losses compound: answers get blander, rare facts vanish, errors repeat.

Research suggests mixing in real human data and filtering carefully prevents most of it. That's why fresh, human-made data has become valuable (see synthetic data, write-up 39).

## Exercise

Explain model collapse to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for model collapse? *Model collapse is AI learning from AI, and fading the same way.*
2. What is it not? *It doesn't mean AI is about to collapse. It's a risk that careful data mixing largely avoids.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Debates about AI-generated text and images filling the web, and AI companies licensing human-written content.*

## Glossary

- **Model collapse:** quality loss from training on AI-made data.
- **Data provenance:** knowing where data came from.
- **Human data:** content created by people.
