# Lesson 23.47: TPU (course companion)

*Explain It Like Hello World. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this lesson, you can explain TPU with a hello-world example, say how it really works in one step, and name its most common misunderstanding.

## The hello world, to retell

A Swiss army knife does many jobs. A bread knife does one job, cutting bread, and does it very well.

## One step further

AI is mostly multiplying big grids of numbers. TPUs (tensor processing units) are built around exactly that, packed into large connected groups for training and serving big models.

Google first used them in its own data centres around 2015 and now rents them through Google Cloud. Other companies build similar custom chips, such as AWS's Trainium and Inferentia.

## Exercise

Explain TPU to someone non-technical in under one minute, using your own everyday example (not the one from the article). Then name one product where they have already met it.

## Quiz

1. What is the hello-world example for TPU? *A TPU is Google's bread knife for AI maths.*
2. What is it not? *A TPU isn't something you buy for your desk. It's rented in the cloud. And it isn't automatically faster than a GPU for every job.*
3. Name one place you've already met it. *Google Search, Translate and Gemini run on TPUs. You meet them indirectly every time you use those.*

## Glossary

- **TPU:** Google's custom chip for AI maths.
- **Tensor:** a grid of numbers, the basic unit of AI maths.
- **Custom silicon:** chips designed for one company's needs.
