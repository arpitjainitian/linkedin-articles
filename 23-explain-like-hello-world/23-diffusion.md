# 23. How Does an AI Draw a Picture From Noise? | Diffusion

![Cover](images/23-diffusion-cover.png)

*Explain It Like Hello World series: Diffusion*

"Diffusion models." The tech behind most AI image and video generators. It sounds like a chemistry lesson.

## The hello world

Take a clear photo of a cat. Add a little static. Then more. Then more, until it's pure TV snow.

Now teach an AI to go **backwards**: remove a little static at a time. That's the hello world: a **diffusion model** starts from noise and cleans it, step by step, into a picture.

## One step further

During training, the AI sees millions of images being turned into noise and learns to predict the noise so it can remove it.

To create something new, it starts from fresh random noise and repeats the cleaning dozens of times. Your text prompt, "a cat in a spacesuit", steers each step, so the picture that emerges matches the words.

## Where you've already met it

AI image generators and the newer AI video tools. "Remove background" and "fill in this area" photo features often use the same idea.

## The real name

**Diffusion models**.

- **Noise:** random static, like a detuned TV.
- **Denoising:** removing noise a little at a time.
- **Prompt guidance:** the text steering each cleaning step.

## What it's not

The AI isn't searching for an existing picture and copying it. But it learned from existing images, which is why copyright and artist consent are real debates.

## Take this to your next meeting

1. Write prompts that describe what you want to see, not instructions.
2. Check hands, text and small details; they still go wrong.
3. Know your company's rules on AI-made images and their sources.

Start with static. Clean it, step by step, into a picture.
