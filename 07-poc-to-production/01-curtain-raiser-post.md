Ten refund requests. All read perfectly. The room clapped. "When can we go live?"

Then request eleven arrived: a blurry photo of a cracked box, with the order number cut off.

Treat a great demo as a production plan, and you pay for it:
• Accuracy: real requests are photos, typos and three languages
• Availability: nobody on call when it breaks on the Monday after a sale
• Scale: 50,000 return requests at once, after Black Friday
• Continuity: no plan B when the AI is down during the returns rush

Write-up 1 in my new series, It worked in the demo (#ItWorkedInTheDemo): why a POC and production are different worlds, what sits between the applause and go-live, and three things to take to your next kickoff meeting.
