# Series 3: "AI transformation = buy licenses + train people"

**Theme:** Buying AI is a purchase. Changing how work gets done is a transformation.

**Level:** L1. Written so anyone can follow it, with no domain knowledge needed.

**Running example:** **A neighbourhood bakery going AI.** The owner buys AI tools for 10 staff: orders, stock, social media, accounts. A business everyone understands.

**Hashtag:** #BeyondTheLicence (proposal)

## Planned write-ups

| # | Title | The hook | What it covers | Example moment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why Did We Buy AI for Everyone and Nothing Changed? | Ten licences, one training session, zero change. | Curtain raiser: tools vs transformation | Staff use AI to write birthday wishes, nothing else | Planned |
| 2 | Which Problem Was AI Supposed to Solve? | "We need AI" is not a problem statement. | Starting from pain points, not tools | Wasted bread every evening, but AI was bought for social posts | Planned |
| 3 | Why Is the Old Way Still Running Next to the New One? | Two processes cost more than one. | Process redesign, retiring the old way | Orders taken by AI and still copied into the notebook | Planned |
| 4 | Who Is Afraid of the AI, and Why Didn't Anyone Ask? | Silent resistance kills more AI projects than bad models. | Fear, trust, involving people early | The senior baker quietly ignores every AI suggestion | Planned |
| 5 | Did the Training Teach Tools or Habits? | A two-hour session doesn't change a twenty-year habit. | Practical, role-based learning, champions | Everyone learned prompts; nobody learned when to use them | Planned |
| 6 | How Do You Know It's Working? | "Everyone uses it" isn't a result. | Baselines, outcome metrics, honest ROI | Less waste? Fewer stock-outs? Nobody measured before | Planned |
| 7 | Who Owns AI After the Launch Party? | Every tool needs an owner, or it becomes shelfware. | Ownership, governance, simple rules for safe use | Nobody knows who can change the AI's settings | Planned |
| 8 | So, Is Your Organisation Transforming or Just Subscribing? | Seven write-ups, one checklist. | Finale: the transformation checklist | The bakery, six months later | Planned |

## Format (same as #JustAddRAG)

- Curiosity-question titles, numbered from 1, "write-up" naming, no fixed schedule.
- Start with why the topic matters; plain-language analogies with technical names revealed at the end; light "Fix:" pointers.
- Polite humor, one very simple running example, cheat sheet at the end, three kickoff takeaways.
- Post text in a copy-paste block, with the "you pay for it" impact of the wrong choice.
- Cover: its own iceberg plus an original meme scene and a sticky-note punchline.
