# 0. Can a Parenting App Teach You the Claude Architect Exam? | The Exam, Mapped

![Cover](images/00-curtain-raiser-cover.png)

*Claude Architect, One Objective at a Time: the curtain raiser*

I'm preparing for the **Claude Certified Architect - Foundations** exam. This series is my study notes, written so they help you pass too.

The exam doesn't ask "what does this setting do?". It asks: *a real system is misbehaving; what would an architect do?* So we need a real system.

Meet **ParentPace**, a made-up company that coaches parents: how to stay calm instead of yelling, talk so children listen, and look after themselves. It has an AI coach, human coaches, a research team, a pile of intake forms and a six-person tech team.

Every write-up shows one exam objective going wrong at ParentPace, then going right.

## The exam, in one look

- **60 questions** in **120 minutes**, multiple choice and multiple response.
- **4 scenarios**, picked at random from a bank of 6.
- **Pass mark:** 720 out of 1,000.
- **5 domains, 30 objectives:**
  - Agentic architecture and orchestration: 27%
  - Claude Code configuration and workflows: 20%
  - Prompt engineering and structured output: 20%
  - Tool design and MCP: 18%
  - Context management and reliability: 15%

## How this series works

- **One write-up per objective.** 30 in all, in the exam guide's order.
- **Every write-up:** a ParentPace moment, how it really works, one exam trap, and one practice question.
- **A coverage table at the end** lists every knowledge and skill statement in that objective, and where it was covered. Nothing skipped.
- **After each domain:** a checkpoint with exam-style questions.
- **At the end:** a mock exam and a one-page exam-day sheet.

## A taste of what's coming

- How does an agent know when to stop? (Hint: not when it says "All done!")
- The prompt said "check the plan first". The agent didn't.
- "I'm close to breaking point." Should the AI keep coaching?

## Words to know

- **Objective (task statement):** one skill the exam tests, like "design agentic loops".
- **Scenario:** a realistic system the questions are built around.
- **Agent:** an AI that uses tools in a loop until a job is done.

## Take this to your exam prep

1. Read the official exam guide once, end to end.
2. Pick one objective a day. Read, then answer the practice question without peeking.
3. Keep a list of the traps you fell for. Revise those, not the ones you got right.

Exams reward judgement, not memory. Let's build some.
