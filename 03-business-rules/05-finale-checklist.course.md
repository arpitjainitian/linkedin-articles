# Lesson 3.5: The Finale Checklist (course companion)

*Module 3: Rules. Not for LinkedIn.*

## Learning objective

After this module, you can audit any AI assistant that applies business rules and decide what must move from the prompt to code.

## The story

Use [05-finale-checklist.md](05-finale-checklist.md): the refund assistant, fixed.

## Deep dive

**The three-way split.** Code (decisions), model (language), people (meaning, exceptions, changes). Most failures come from blurring one of these lines: the model deciding, code holding rules nobody owns, or people changing rules without a trace.

**Mapping to the module.** Decisions in code and untouched numbers (3.2); policy to rules and vague words (3.3); one place and dated changes (3.4); proof links to decision lineage (Today in AI No. 006).

## Capstone lab

1. Take the refund assistant from this module.
2. Score it with the checklist.
3. Fix the two lowest items; re-score; write a one-page "rules ownership" note: who owns which rules, how changes happen.

## Quiz

1. Name the three parties in the split. *Code, the AI, people.*
2. What does a score under 5 mean? *The AI is effectively making policy.*
3. What's the quickest first fix? *Move one rule out of the prompt into the rules store.*
